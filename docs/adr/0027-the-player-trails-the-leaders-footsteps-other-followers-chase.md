# A following Player replays the leader's footsteps; any other follower chases by shortest path

Refines `adr/0025`. grid-engine's `follow` doesn't walk the leader's route. Each time the leader moves, it sends the follower along the shortest path to any tile next to the leader. That is fine for an NPC tracking the Player. But a manual playtest of a Player following the Guard around a small loop showed the Player cutting across the inside of the loop and ending up in the Guard's way, so the loop never closed. A Player being led somewhere should walk exactly where they're led. So the follow's style depends on who follows:
- **The Player as follower** replays the leader's footsteps. The Engine records each tile the leader leaves (on grid-engine's `positionChangeFinished`, because the exit tile stays blocked until then) and walks the Player into them one at a time, `gap` footsteps behind, so the Player can never cut a corner or step into the leader's path.
- **A Character Entity as follower** keeps grid-engine's shortest-path chase, with `gap` as its `distance`.

We rejected making every follower trail, because grid-engine's chase is good enough for NPCs and trailing is custom code. We also rejected a per-step option the author picks, because nothing needs the Player to chase or an NPC to trail. A trail is the Engine's own subscription, not a grid-engine movement, so `adr/0025`'s mechanism note no longer holds for it: a new Movement step on the Player doesn't replace a trail by itself. The Engine therefore cancels any trail on a character whenever that character is given a Movement step or is stopped. `adr/0025`'s rule stands unchanged: a Follow step never holds the Cutscene, and every follow ends when its Cutscene ends or when the follower is given its own Movement step.
