# Spawning inside a Zone counts as entering it, on every Map load — not just the game's first boot

Zone's trigger is edge-triggered: it fires on the transition from outside a Zone's rectangle to inside it, not continuously while the Player stands there. The obvious reading of "edge-triggered" would exclude the Player's spawn position ever counting, since spawning isn't a movement-driven transition — under that reading, a Zone placed over a Spawn Point would simply never fire until the Player left and re-entered. We rejected that reading: before the Player exists in the world at all, they trivially aren't inside any Zone, so the moment they first materialize on a Map — including right after the boot CG ends, since there's no Player to place until then — is itself the first outside→inside transition, not a special case bolted onto the rule. This applies uniformly at every Map (re)load, not only the game's first boot: walking through a Portal or an edge-transition into a Map whose own Spawn Point sits inside a Zone fires it too, the same as if the Player had walked there directly. The once-ever-per-Student Flag gate (`runOnce`) already prevents a repeat visit from replaying it, so this rule needs no extra state of its own to stay correct.
