# A Follow step starts following and moves on, instead of holding the Cutscene until it resolves

Amends `adr/0017`, whose step-runner holds execution at each step until that step's completion event arrives. grid-engine's `follow` never completes: it keeps the follower trailing the leader indefinitely. So a Follow step that waited like every other step would hang the Cutscene forever. Instead, the runner starts the follow and advances straight away, and the next step (usually a Movement step walking the leader) is what the Cutscene actually waits on. There is no explicit stop step. A follow ends when its Cutscene ends (the runner stops every follow it started, so control never returns to a Player still trailing someone) or when the follower is given its own Movement step (grid-engine replaces a character's movement when it gets a new one). We rejected an explicit `stopFollow` step because no scene needs to stop mid-Cutscene yet, and it can be added later without changing this rule. We also rejected tying the follow to the leader's next Movement step, because that couples two steps' lifetimes in a way an author can't see from the step list.
